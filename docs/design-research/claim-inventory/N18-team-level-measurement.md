- **N18:1** (argument, A, field) The instruments that measure a software team fall into three families (developer-productivity frameworks, team-cognition instruments, AI-era surveys) that do not share a level of analysis, and none of them measures a shared model of a codebase. From: [Finding](../team-level-measurement.md#finding).
- **N18:2** (argument, A, field) The developer-productivity frameworks are frameworks, not questionnaires. From: [Finding](../team-level-measurement.md#finding).
- **N18:3** (argument, A, field) The team-cognition instruments are older and validated on more teams than the developer-productivity frameworks. From: [Finding](../team-level-measurement.md#finding).
- **N18:4** (finding, -, field) Under AI adoption, every survey with a published instrument is scored at the individual. Source: Butler 2024; Choudhuri 2025; Annunziata 2026. From: [Finding](../team-level-measurement.md#finding).
- **N18:5** (argument, A, field) The SPACE article is argument and framework; it reports no study. Source: Forsgren et al. 2021. From: [SPACE (2021): a framework, not a questionnaire](../team-level-measurement.md#space-2021-a-framework-not-a-questionnaire).
- **N18:6** (definition, A, team) SPACE names five dimensions (satisfaction and well-being; performance; activity; communication and collaboration; efficiency and flow) and three levels of measure: individual, team or group, and system. Source: Forsgren et al. 2021. From: [SPACE (2021): a framework, not a questionnaire](../team-level-measurement.md#space-2021-a-framework-not-a-questionnaire).
