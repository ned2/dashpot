- **N09:1** (definition, A, team, person) A transactive memory system is "a set of individual memory systems in combination with the communication that takes place between individuals"; "Other people can be locations of external storage for the individual." Source: Wegner 1987. From: [Wegner 1987: the definition, and two ways responsibility is allocated](../expert-finding-and-transactive-memory.md#wegner-1987-the-definition-and-two-ways-responsibility-is-allocated). Placement: -/-/- X9 G15 T3.
- **N09:2** (mechanism, A, team, person) What each person holds about the others is a directory of labels and locations, not the content; retrieval is "transactive" because it goes through the person who holds the item. Source: Wegner 1987. From: [Wegner 1987: the definition, and two ways responsibility is allocated](../expert-finding-and-transactive-memory.md#wegner-1987-the-definition-and-two-ways-responsibility-is-allocated). Placement: W6/D7/- G15 T3.
- **N09:3** (mechanism, A, team, person) Responsibility for a domain is assigned "either through personal expertise, or through the circumstantial knowledge responsibility"; "The person who most recently encounters a domain of information may also incur responsibility for it". Source: Wegner 1987. From: [Wegner 1987: the definition, and two ways responsibility is allocated](../expert-finding-and-transactive-memory.md#wegner-1987-the-definition-and-two-ways-responsibility-is-allocated).
